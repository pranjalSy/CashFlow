3. Ho gaya

Step 1: saaf list ki kya hatega (entries, udhar, EMI/bills, log, saare phones sign out), phone aur cloud dono se, wapas nahi aata. Upar "Export your data first" (screen 22). Premium wale ko yaad: Google Play ki subscription Play Store se cancel karo, account delete karne se billing band nahi hoti. "Keep my account" se wapas.
Step 2: "Delete" type karo (galti se na ho; chhota/bada letter koi bhi chalega), phir PIN ya fingerprint (koi aur aadmi phone uthakar delete na kar sake). Tab tak "Delete forever" laal par kaam nahi karta, dabane par message.
Step 3: server par saara data hatta hai, phone ka local data aur keys bhi mit jaati hain. "Account deleted" screen, phir app band / Welcome screen.
Offline ho to: delete ke liye internet zaroori; "Connect to the internet to delete your account".
Backups: purane backup (H3) mein bhi 7 din mein hat jaata hai; ye privacy policy mein bhi likhna hoga.
Turant delete (owner decision): koi waiting time / undo nahi. Delete forever dabate hi account aur data hat jata hai.
