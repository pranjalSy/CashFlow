On hone ke baad

Sirf fingerprint: phone mein fingerprint sensor na ho to ye screen aati hi nahi (PIN se hi khulega). Face unlock abhi nahi.
Turn on: Android ka apna fingerprint popup khulta hai (app fingerprint kabhi nahi dekhta). Sahi touch par green tick, ring halka sa phailta hai.
Animation: fingerprint ke peeche ki rings dheere dheere "saans" leti hain (sirf scale + opacity, low-end par band).
Not now: seedha aage; baad mein Settings se on kar sakte hain.
