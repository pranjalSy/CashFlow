Contacts permission nahi di

Upar 2 card: To receive (blue: log aapko denge) aur To pay (pink: aapko dena hai), kitne logon ke saath. Tap = sirf wahi log.
Recent: jin logon ke saath haal mein udhar hua, max 5 (mockup mein jagah ke liye kam dikhaye). Har row: naam, due date ("Due in 3 days" 7 din se kam ho to orange), aur net amount + "to receive / to pay". Sab chukta ho to "Settled".
All contacts: phone ke contacts A–Z, right side letter index (sirf is section mein dikhta hai) se jump. Contacts sirf phone par padhe jaate hain.
Tap kisi bhi person par: Person detail (agli screen), jisme uski saari entries.
"+": wahi chhota menu (Expense, Income, Lent, Borrowed). Contacts off ho to Lent/Borrowed mein naam aur number khud likh sakte hain.
