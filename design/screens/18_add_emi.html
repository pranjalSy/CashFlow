Fields: Name (+ icon), Lender (optional), Item price, Down payment (optional), Loan amount (apne aap = price − down payment, ya seedha loan amount likho), Interest yearly %, First EMI date (EMI har mahine isi tareekh; 29/30/31 ho to mahine ka aakhri din).
"I know the": Months ya EMI amount, ek daalo, doosra turant calculate. Dono baad mein edit ho sakte hain.
Result card: EMI/month (ya kitne months), total interest, total to pay, aakhri EMI. Number badalte hi live update.
Calculation: reducing balance. ₹90,000, 12%, 12 months = ₹7,996.39 (brief ka reference). 0% interest = loan ÷ months. EMI se months nikalne par aakhri EMI chhoti.
Not possible: EMI monthly interest se kam ho to laal card aur saaf wajah. Save button grey nahi hota (app ka rule); dabane par card halka hilta hai aur save nahi hota.
Save: saari installments apne aap ban jati hain; EMI list mein progress "0 of 12 paid".
