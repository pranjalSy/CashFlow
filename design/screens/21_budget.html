Kahan se: Charts screen ke upar "Budget" card (aur Settings mein bhi).
Upar card: is mahine kitna kharch, kul limit, ring mein %. Neeche "₹6,500 left · 17 days · ₹382/day" (roz kitna kharch kar sakte ho). Month picker se pichle mahine bhi dekho.
Rang ka rule (brief): 80% se kam green, 80–99% orange, 100%+ laal + "₹800 over".
Category limits: har category ki apni limit aur bar. Tap = limit sheet. "Add category limit" se nayi.
Limit sheet (solid white): amount number pad se, suggestions (pichle 3 mahine ka average, round numbers), "Alert me" on/off, Remove, Save. Limit har mahine same rehti hai.
Pehli baar: koi budget nahi to ek card "Set a monthly budget" + pichle 3 mahine ke average ka suggestion.
Alerts: 80% aur 100% par ek ek notification (ek mahine mein ek baar hi). "Hide amounts in notifications" on ho to amount nahi dikhta.
Kya ginte hain: sirf Expense entries (udhar nahi, EMI/bill payment expense ke roop mein gine jaate hain).
