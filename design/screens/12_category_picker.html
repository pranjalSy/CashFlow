Sheet: solid safed (keyboard ki tarah), neeche se spring ke saath. Neeche kheench kar band.
Recent: sabse zyada use wali 4 categories upar, ek tap = chuno aur sheet band.
Grid: 4 column, har category ka apna colour aur icon, taaki list mein jaldi pehchaan ho. Chuni hui category par type ka colour (Expense laal, Income green).
Search: naam ke kisi bhi hisse se ("gro" = Groceries). Na mile to "Create “Gym”": ek tap mein nayi category ban jati hai, icon/colour apne aap (baad mein badal sakte hain), aur agli baar search/recent mein milti hai.
