Income · sab bhara hua

Ek hi screen: Expense aur Income dono mein amount, category aur saari details ek saath. Screen khulte hi amount active hota hai aur number pad neeche se aata hai; "Done" ya form par tap karte hi pad neeche chala jata hai. Indian comma apne aap (45,000 / 1,20,000). Paise ke liye "." button.
Expense | Income switch: poori screen ka colour badalta hai (laal ↔ green), sign bhi (− / +). Galti se galat type chun liya ho to ek tap mein badlo.
Category: amount ke neeche chip. Chip par tap = category picker (agli screen), jisme recent categories upar aur "+ New" se nayi (apne aap save).
Title (optional): khaali chhodo to category hi title ban jati hai.
Compulsory: sirf Amount aur Paid via / Received in (Cash ya Online). Payment chuna nahi aur Save dabaya to dono buttons laal + "Choose how you paid". Button kabhi grey/disabled nahi, taaki user ko pata chale kya bacha hai.
Date / Time: optional. Na chuna to abhi ka date-time apne aap ("Now" tag). Tap = chhota calendar/time sheet.
Paid via: Expense aur Income dono mein same: Cash ya Online; Online chuno to UPI / Card / Bank (default UPI). Income par label "Received in".
Save: button type ke colour ka. Save hote hi chhota tick animation, Home par entry upar slide hokar aati hai. Edit mode mein upar "Delete" bhi (confirm glass sheet).
