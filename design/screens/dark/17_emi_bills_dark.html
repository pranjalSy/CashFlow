Switch: EMIs | Bills. (Recurring income hata diya, owner decision; salary normal Income entry se.)
Upar card: is mahine kitna dena hai aur agla kab.
EMI card: naam, bank, rate, monthly EMI; progress bar "4 of 12 paid · 8 left", "₹63,971 remaining", due tag (7 din se kam = orange). Tap = EMI detail (saari installments, Mark paid).
Bills (naya flow, I3): har bill "every N months" ke saath. Is mahine wale upar, baaki "Later". Card par pichle bill ka amount; Paid par pichle 3 amount chips (screen 20). Due today = orange solid tag, Paid = green.
"Paid or not?" popup: due ke din reminder aata hai; app kholne par ye popup. Paid = expense apne aap ban jata hai aur EMI ki ek installment kam (ek saath, ek hi action). Not yet = agli baar app kholne par phir poochega. Bill ke Paid par amount pichle 3 bill ke chips se (screen 20).
"+" (is tab par): sirf Add EMI aur Add bill. Lent/Borrowed/Expense/Income wale 4 buttons sirf baaki tabs par.
Free user: Charts jaisa locked view + Try Premium.
Numbers: ₹90,000, 12%, 12 mahine = EMI ₹7,996.39; 4 paid ke baad ₹63,971 remaining (brief ke reference).
