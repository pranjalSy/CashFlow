"+" dabane par: chhota menu, right side

Header: avatar par tap = Settings. Greeting time ke hisaab se (Good morning / afternoon / evening). Month picker se poori screen us mahine ki ho jati hai (swipe left/right bhi).
Summary card: bada number = mahine ke end ka balance; neeche Cash aur Online alag. 👁 se amounts chhupa sakte hain (privacy, jaise kisi ke saamne phone khola ho).
4 tiles: Income green, Expense red, Lent blue, Borrowed pink. Tile par tap = us type ki list.
List: Income, Expense aur Udhar (Lent/Borrowed) sab. Har entry alag card, beech mein 8px space. Udhar entry par tap = us person ki detail screen. Din ke hisaab se group. Card: icon, title, "category · payment · time", amount. Title lamba ho to "..." par amount kabhi nahi katta.
Bottom bar: glass pill: Home (naam ke saath), Udhar, Charts, EMI & Bills. Tab badalne par white pill spring se slide. Alag blue "+" button.
"+" menu: "+" ke theek upar right side mein chhota glass menu (neeche se ~90–300px): Expense, Income, Lent, Borrowed. "+" se nikal kar khulta hai (scale + har item 30ms ke gap par), "+" ghoom kar "×". Bahar tap = band. Angutha seedha pahunchta hai.
Animation: month badalne par numbers count-up; list ke pehle 5 items halka fade-in; scroll karne par header glass ban jata hai.
