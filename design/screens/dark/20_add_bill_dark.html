4. Yes: amount ek tap mein + payment

Step 1, Bill type: Electricity, Gas, Mobile, Internet, DTH / Cable, Water, Insurance, Other. Har type ka apna icon aur rang. Neeche "Popular" providers (Torrent Power, Adani Total Gas…) jinka gap pehle se bhara (2 months).
Step 2, Form: provider ka naam, Customer ID (optional, sirf yaad ke liye), Comes every 1 / 2 / 3 / 6 / 12 months, Next due date. Neeche preview: "Then 15 Jan, 15 Mar, 15 May…" taaki user ko pakka pata ho. Usual amount optional, Remind me (on due day / 3 / 7 days before).
Popup + notification: due ke din (aur reminder wale din) notification; app kholne par popup "Did you pay this bill?". Not yet = kal phir poochega (jab tak pay na ho).
Yes, paid: pichle 3 bill ke amount suggestion mein (mahine ke naam ke saath), sabse naya pehle se chuna. Sahi ho to bas Save; alag ho to "Other amount" (number pad). Paid via compulsory (pichli baar wala chuna). Save = Expense · Bills category mein entry, ek saath.
Pehli baar: suggestion nahi hote, to "Usual amount" (agar daala) chip mein, warna seedha number pad.
Agla bill: pay hote hi agli due date apne aap (15 Nov + 2 months = 15 Jan). Due date ek baar badli to aage ki dates usi se.
Pehle wale design se badla: Monthly/Yearly switch ki jagah "Comes every N months"; "Same amount" switch hata diya (suggestions se kaam ho jata hai).
