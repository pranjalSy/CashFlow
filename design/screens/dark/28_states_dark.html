Jahan internet zaroori hai

Empty (koi data nahi): har list wali screen par ek hi style: chhota icon, ek line kya karna hai, ek button. Home par "+" ki taraf arrow (sirf pehli baar). Opening balance (₹12,000) summary mein dikhta hai. Contacts, Charts, Bills, Search ke empty bhi isi style mein.
Locked (Free user): tab dikhta hai par peeche blur, upar card: kya milega + Try Premium (amber). Charts (16) aur EMI & Bills dono same. Charts (16) ka lock bhi ab amber.
Offline: app poora offline chalta hai: entry add / edit / delete sab phone par save. Upar chhoti grey patti "Offline · 2 changes saved on phone". Jo entries abhi sync nahi hui unpar chhota ghadi icon. Koi popup ya rukawat nahi.
Wapas online: apne aap sync, neeche green toast "Back online · all synced" 2 sec, ghadi icons hat jaate hain.
Internet zaroori: sirf login, naye phone ka approval, Premium kharidna, delete account. Wahan "No internet" + Try again, aur saaf likha ki entries offline chalti hain.
Mockup note: summary card yahan chhota dikhaya hai; app mein Home (10) wala poora card.
