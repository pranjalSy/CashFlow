Kho gaya phone sign out

Rule (brief 5.10): naye phone par login pending rehta hai aur koi data nahi dikhta jab tak purana phone approve na kare. Ye check server par hota hai, sirf app mein nahi.
Purana phone: notification "New login from Redmi Note 12. Was this you?". Tap = app khulta hai (pehle PIN/fingerprint), phir ye popup. Yes = naya phone approve, data sync shuru. No = naya phone sign out, kuch nahi dikhta, aur Devices mein "Blocked" record.
Naya phone: "Approve this phone" screen, kaunse phone par jaana hai uska naam, 10 minute ka timer (khatam = phir se login). Approve hote hi apne aap aage (Home + data).
Email code: purana phone kho gaya / band hai to 6-digit code email par. 15 minute valid, 5 try. Email aadha chhupa (pr•••••@gmail.com).
Devices list: saare logged-in phones, "This phone" tag, last active. Remove = email code ke baad us phone ka sign out. Neeche pichli requests: Approved / Blocked.
Phone kho gaya (owner idea): naye phone par email code se login karte hi sheet "Lost your old phone?" aur baaki phones ki list. Kisi par Sign out = email par naya code, code daalo, wo phone turant sign out. Wo phone jab bhi internet se judega, server mana karega aur app us phone ka saara data mita dega. Tab tak data encrypted aur PIN ke peeche.
Devices list se Remove: kisi bhi phone se karo, hamesha email code maangta hai (koi chura kar baaki phones band na kar sake).
Pehla phone: account ka pehla phone apne aap trusted (koi approval nahi).
Privacy: sirf device ka naam aur Android version; location nahi dikhate.
