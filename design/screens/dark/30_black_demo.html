3 · Proper black + glass#000 + glow

1 · Navy black: background #111827, cards thode halke. Shant, aankhon ko aaram.
2 · Proper black: background #000 (OLED phone par pixel band = battery bachat), cards #111114. Zyada contrast, Apple jaisa.
3 · Proper black + glass: peeche halki brand roshni (blue, purple, pink glow) jo upar se neeche fade hoti hai. Summary card, entries, chips, header buttons aur bottom bar frosted glass; "+" button par chamak. Sabse premium aur attractive.
3 ke saath dhyaan: glass ke liye ek rule badalna hoga (abhi "amounts sirf solid par", B1). Amount padhne mein saaf rahe isliye glass gehra rakha hai, aur "Reduce glass effects" / low-end phone par apne aap solid (style 2). Blur zyada hone se purane phones par thoda bhaari, isliye ek screen par blur layers limited rakhenge.
