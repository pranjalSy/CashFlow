"Choose person" par tap: person picker

Ek hi screen: Add Expense jaisa. Upar Lent (blue) | Borrowed (pink) switch, poora colour badalta hai.
Compulsory: Person, Amount, aur Paid via (Lent) / Received in (Borrowed). Kuch bacha ho to Save par laal border + saaf message.
Person: Person detail se aaye to naam pehle se ("Change" se badlo). "+" se aaye to "Choose person": search, recent log (unka current hisaab bhi dikhta hai), saare contacts, ya "Add new person" (naam + number khud).
Amount: tap = wahi solid number pad. Neeche chhota text "You gave Rahul" / "You took from Rahul", taaki direction galat na ho.
Date: default Now.
Premium (dono): Due date (1 week / 2 weeks / 1 month / Pick date) aur Remind me (Off / 3 days / 7 days before + due ke din). Dono optional.
Free user: dono dikhte hain par 👑 Premium tag ke saath; tap karne par chhota paywall sheet (₹1 mein 3 mahine). Baaki form chalta rahe, entry bina due date save ho jati hai.
Save: chhota tick, wapas Person detail ya Home, entry upar slide hokar.
