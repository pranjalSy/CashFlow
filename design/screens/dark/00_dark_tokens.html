Rules:
• Owner choice: Proper black + glass. Background #000 (OLED par battery bachat), upar halki brand roshni (blue, purple, pink), neeche halka blue glow.
• Cards = frosted glass (summary, entries, lists, search, chips). Sheets, popups, number pad, menus = solid #141418, taaki amount aur buttons hamesha saaf.
• Amount ke rang (green/red/blue/pink) dark par halke kiye taaki padhne mein saaf (contrast 4.5+).
• Buttons par safed text hota hai, isliye button ke rang gehre rakhe (#15803D, #DC2626, #4263EB, #B45309), safed text ka contrast 4.5+.
• Coloured cards (Balance, EMI hero) wahi brand blue; unke andar safed text aur safed progress bar.
• Bottom bar: dark glass pill, active tab halka glass; "+" button gradient + glow.
• Purane phone / "Reduce glass effects": glass ki jagah solid #111114 (same layout).
• Theme: Settings → Theme (System / Light / Dark). Default System.
