Paisa dena hai: "Did you pay it back?"

Kab: udhar ki due date wale din (aur 3 / 7 din pehle wala reminder agar on ho). Notification par tap ya app kholte hi popup. Ek se zyada due ho to ek ke baad ek.
3 jawab:
 • Got full / Paid full: poora baaki amount repayment ban jata hai, udhar Settled. Bas payment type (Cash/Online) ek tap mein poochta hai.
 • Got some / Paid some: amount daalo (number pad popup ke andar), Cash/Online chuno, Save. Baaki amount dikhta hai; due date wahi rakho ya badlo.
 • Not yet: popup band; agle din phir poochega. Saath mein "change the due date" link se nayi date (purana record history mein).
Zyada mila / diya: "Got some" mein baaki se zyada daala to wahi pehle wala rule: Settled + snackbar "Add as borrowed/lent?".
Premium: due date Premium hai, isliye ye popup bhi sirf Premium users ko.
