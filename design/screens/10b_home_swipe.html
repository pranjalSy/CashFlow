Kaise kaam karta hai

  1. Normal: card ke peeche kuch nahi dikhta.
  2. Thoda kheencho: Edit aur Delete buttons ungli ke saath dheere dheere bade hote hain (icon bhi grow hota hai).
  3. Aadhe se kam chhodo: card spring se wapas.
  4. Thoda zyada chhodo: card khula rehta hai, dono buttons dikhte hain.
  5. Bahut zor se / poora kheencho (60%+): Delete button poora phail jata hai, halka haptic, chhodte hi confirm sheet "Delete this entry?" (Cancel / Delete). Delete button par tap karne par bhi yahi sheet.
  6. Delete dabaya: entry hatti hai + neeche Undo (5 sec). Cancel: card wapas apni jagah.
  7. Udhar entry par tap (Rahul, Amit): us person ki detail screen khulti hai.
  8. "+" dabao: right side mein chhota glass menu (Expense, Income, Lent, Borrowed). Bahar tap = band.
